# Action-image provenance

Every image in this directory depicts the named player during the specific
postseason identified by its filename. Source-page and direct-image URLs are
recorded in `action_shot_manifest.csv`.

The images are official NBA.com editorial images or NBA.com classic-game
thumbnails. Several underlying photographs are credited by NBA.com to NBAE or
Getty Images. They are included here as sourced editorial references; this
project does **not** assert that their copyrights or republication rights have
been transferred. Confirm the applicable permission before publishing the
graphic externally. The chart remains reproducible with initials or the
separately documented portrait fallbacks if these files are omitted.
